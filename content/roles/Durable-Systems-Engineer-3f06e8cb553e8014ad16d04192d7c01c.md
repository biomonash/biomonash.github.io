## Duties & Responsibilities

- Assist with the research and development of internal structures and external encasements in your allocated project.
- Contribute to CAD development, prototyping, fabrication, testing, and refinement of durable system components.
- Communicate project requirements, progress, and issues with the Durable Systems Lead and relevant project teams.

## What we’re looking for

- Familiarity with CAD software, material properties, and basic fabrication methods.
- An aptitude for practical problem-solving and translating design concepts into functional physical components.
- Reliable and consistent communication, with a willingness to collaborate and contribute to a team environment.
