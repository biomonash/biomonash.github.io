## Duties & Responsibilities

- Lead the research and development of internal structures and external encasements across various BiOM projects.
- Request and mobilise sub-team purchases relevant to various BiOM projects.
- Collect and organise various resources to ensure seamless leadership transition.

## What we’re looking for

- Familiarity with CAD software, material properties, and fabrication methods.
- An aptitude to lead and/or real experience leading a team or project: planning timelines, supporting members, allocating resources and resolving interpersonal conflicts.
- Reliable and consistent communications between leadership and sub-team members.
