## Duties & Responsibilities

- Assist with the research and development of signal processing and sustainable stand-alone power systems in your allocated project.
- Contribute to circuit design, component selection, prototyping, testing, and integration of electronic systems.
- Communicate project requirements, progress, and issues with the Electronics Systems Lead and relevant project teams.

## What we’re looking for

- Familiarity with circuit design, electronic components, microcontrollers, PCB design, and basic manufacturing processes.
- An aptitude for practical problem-solving and translating system requirements into functional electronic solutions.
- Reliable and consistent communication, with a willingness to collaborate and contribute to a team environment.
