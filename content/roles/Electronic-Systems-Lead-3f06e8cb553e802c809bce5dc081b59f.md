## Duties & Responsibilities

- Lead the research and development of signal processing and sustainable stand alone power systems across various BiOM projects.
- Request and mobilise sub-team purchases relevant to various BiOM projects.
- Collect and organise various resources and documents to ensure seamless leadership transition.

## What we’re looking for

- Familiarity with system architecture, circuit design, component selection, PCB design, and manufacturing processes.
- An aptitude to lead and/or real experience leading a team or project: planning timelines, supporting members, allocating resources and resolving interpersonal conflicts.
- Reliable and consistent communications between leadership and sub-team members.
