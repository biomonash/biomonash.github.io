## Duties & Responsibilities

- Lead the research and development across all of BiOM’s engineering sub-teams and manage the technical efforts made towards conservation projects.
- Make/escalate purchase requests, complaints, suggestions, and unresolved problems with senior BiOM leadership.
- Collect and organise various resources and documents to ensure seamless leadership transition.
- Connect and engage with internal and external stakeholders including academic advisors.

## What we’re looking for

- Familiarity with BiOM values, vision, and mission.
- An aptitude to lead and/or real experience leading a range of teams or projects: planning timelines, supporting members, allocating resources and resolving interpersonal conflicts.
- Reliable and consistent communications between leadership and sub-team members.
