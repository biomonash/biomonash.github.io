## Duties & Responsibilities

- Assist with the research and development of embedded systems and digital ecosystems across in your allocated project.
- Contribute to software development, system integration, testing, and troubleshooting across BiOM projects.
- Communicate project requirements, progress, and issues with the Software Integration Lead and relevant project teams.

## What we’re looking for

- Familiarity with embedded programming, software architecture, version control, APIs, and system integration.
- An aptitude for practical problem-solving and developing reliable software solutions for real-world applications.
- Reliable and consistent communication, with a willingness to collaborate and contribute to a team environment.
